Kerala — river and heritage journeys

Meet India, slowly.

Four stops from an old harbour to green tea hills — a journey you take at the pace of the water.

Plan this journey

01

Kochi

The harbour that traded the world.

Fort Kochi grew on spices and sails, and the Chinese fishing nets still lean over its waterfront — working, not posed. Heritage here is not behind glass; it is the street you walk to dinner.

02

Alappuzha — the backwaters

Days that arrive by water.

Kettuvallam houseboats drift the Punnamada and the narrow canals beyond — rice barges turned into river rooms. Much of this world is reached by water and nothing else, which is exactly the point.

03

Periyar — the forest

The forest watches back.

At Thekkady the landscape turns into a lake inside a sanctuary. The boat safari does the quiet work of looking; the morning does the rest.

04

Munnar — the tea hills

Green that climbs.

Tea terraces climb the hills in silence, and the road from Periyar keeps a window seat busy the whole way. The journey ends high, where the air is cold and the view is planted.
